The Competition

Office vs Office

Three offices. Three teams. One challenge. Standerton, Secunda, and Middelburg go head-to-head to create the best marketing videos.

01
Standerton

The original. Show everyone why Standerton sets the standard — bring your A-game and represent your office with pride.

02
Secunda

Energy capital, energy team. Step up and prove that Secunda's juniors have what it takes to steal the show.

03
Middelburg

Don't let the name fool you — there's nothing middle about your ambition. Come out swinging and claim the crown.

✦
The Brief

Two Deliverables

Every office must submit both videos. First, showcase your expertise with an informative piece. Then, entertain us with creativity and personality.

01
Informative

Educate your audience. Showcase VDM's services, story, or what makes us stand out — with clarity, creativity, and polish. 2–5 minutes.

02
Entertainment

Entertain, engage, and surprise us. Show personality, humour, and energy. One video or several shorts — your choice. 2–5 minutes total.

✦
The Rules

What You Need To Know

⏱️
Duration
Both videos required. Each: minimum 2 min, maximum 5 min. Entertainment can be one video or several shorts totalling 2–5 min.
🏢
Participation
All juniors in each office form one team. Everyone participates — no one sits this one out.
🎨
Resources
You may use Chraeten's resources to help produce your video, or go fully DIY. Your call.
🏆
Judging
Videos will be screened in the boardroom during a Wednesday meeting. Non participating staff vote. Winners are announced on the day.
✦
The Deadline
Submit your video by
Friday, 31 July

After submissions close, all videos will be screened in the boardroom during a Wednesday meeting. Non participating staff members will serve as judges — and the winners will be announced live. 🎉
